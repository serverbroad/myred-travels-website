## Visual Language
- Core visual signature: a single red-coral dot-and-line motif repeated as section markers, connecting the idea of routes, dots on a map, and a booking journey.
- Material & depth: layered ivory and white surfaces with very soft diffused shadows; no glassmorphism, no heavy gradients.
- Containers & buttons: large rounded cards with subtle shadows, solid red-coral primary buttons, ghost outline buttons use red-coral 1px border.
- Layout rhythm: generous alternating whitespace between sections, with coral accent distributed as small punctuation (buttons, icons, active dots) rather than large fields.

## Animation
- Entrance: sections fade up and translate-y-6 on scroll into view, 600ms, ease-out.
- Interaction: primary buttons scale 1.03 on hover; service cards lift -2px and shadow deepens on hover.
- Scroll: a subtle horizontal drift on the destinations row when scrolling into view.

## Forbidden
- No purple/blue gradients or generic startup hero gradients.
- No large flat red-coral backgrounds except in the hero area.
- No emoji icons or CSS-faked logos.

## Additional Notes
- All user-visible copy is in English.
- Remove the top navigation menu entirely; place App Store and Google Play badges in the top-right hero area where the menu was removed.
- The site is a single-page landing page.
